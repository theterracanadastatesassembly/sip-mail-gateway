# SIP COMMUNITY USE LICENCE
**SIP-CUL — Version 1.0 (Proposed Draft)**

**Sovereign Infrastructure Project (SIP)**

## 1. Purpose

The SIP Community Use Licence (SIP-CUL) establishes the terms under which materials published by the Sovereign Infrastructure Project (SIP) may be accessed, used, copied, modified and shared.

The purpose of this licence is to encourage the free exchange of knowledge, practical infrastructure resources and technical solutions while preventing the sale or commercial resale of SIP materials as products.

SIP is intended to remain accessible to individuals and Assemblies without requiring payment for the materials themselves.

## 2. Materials Covered

Unless a particular resource states otherwise, this licence applies to the SIP materials distributed with it, including:

- Source code and scripts.
- Software configurations and deployment files.
- Technical manuals and documentation.
- Diagrams, reference designs and instructional materials.
- Modifications and derivative versions of those materials.

Third-party components remain subject to their own applicable licences and terms.

## 3. Free Use and Access

Anyone may, without payment to SIP:

- Access and download SIP materials.
- Install and operate SIP materials for their own purposes.
- Use SIP materials within their own Assembly or organization.
- Make copies for personal, administrative or operational use.
- Modify and adapt SIP materials to suit local requirements.
- Share SIP materials with other individuals or Assemblies.

No registration, membership or payment to SIP is required by this licence.

## 4. Modification and Redistribution

Recipients are permitted to modify, adapt and redistribute SIP materials, provided that:

1. Original authorship and attribution notices are preserved where reasonably practicable.
2. The SIP-CUL terms accompany any redistributed copies.
3. Modifications are identified as modifications and are not falsely represented as original SIP publications.
4. Recipients are not misled into believing that modified materials have been officially reviewed, approved or endorsed by SIP.

Redistribution must remain subject to this licence. Modified versions distributed to others must be provided under the same SIP-CUL terms; recipients may not impose additional restrictions that remove or diminish the permissions granted by this licence.

## 5. Prohibition on Sale and Resale of SIP Materials

SIP materials are provided for free community use and are not offered for sale.

No recipient may:

- Sell SIP materials or copies of SIP materials.
- Charge a purchase price, licence fee or access fee for SIP materials.
- Resell SIP materials, whether in their original or modified form.
- Package SIP materials as a commercial product for sale.
- Restrict access to SIP materials by requiring payment.
- Claim ownership of original SIP materials in order to sell or control their distribution.

This restriction applies to the materials themselves, not to legitimate services performed by individuals or service providers.

## 6. Permitted Paid Services

Nothing in this licence prevents an individual or service provider from charging reasonable fees for work performed in connection with SIP materials.

Permitted services include, but are not limited to:

- Installation and deployment.
- Configuration and customization.
- Infrastructure design and implementation.
- Training and instruction.
- Technical support and troubleshooting.
- Maintenance and administration.
- Hosting and operational management.
- Development of additional tools or services.

A service provider may supply copies of SIP materials as reasonably necessary to perform an installation or other permitted service, provided that no separate charge is made for the SIP materials themselves and the recipient retains the rights granted by this licence.

Payment must be for the work, expertise, time or service provided, not for the purchase or licensing of SIP.

## 7. Attribution and Original Work

Original authorship notices, copyright statements and licence references must not be deliberately removed or misrepresented.

Modifications may identify their own authors and contributors, but must distinguish those contributions from the original SIP material.

No person may claim to be the original author of work created by another.

## 8. Names, Identity and Official Recognition

This licence does not grant permission to use or reproduce:

- Assembly names or official identities.
- Logos, seals, stamps or insignia.
- Official marks or identifying symbols.
- Names or branding associated with SIP or its contributing Assemblies.

Such materials are outside the permissions granted by this licence unless separate permission has been provided by the relevant rights holder.

Permission to use SIP technical materials does not constitute official recognition, representation, partnership or endorsement by SIP or any Assembly.

## 9. No Warranty

SIP materials are provided on an "as is" basis, without guarantees that they will be suitable for a particular purpose, operate without interruption, or be free of defects.

Each recipient is responsible for reviewing, testing, securing and adapting materials before deploying them in their own environment.

To the extent permitted by applicable law, the original authors and contributors disclaim liability for losses or damages arising from the use, modification or deployment of SIP materials.

## 10. Licence Violations and Termination

A recipient who violates the conditions of this licence loses the permissions granted under it while the violation remains unresolved.

Permissions may be restored when the violation has been corrected and the recipient has ceased the unauthorized activity.

Termination does not remove the obligation to respect applicable rights in the original materials.

## 11. Preservation of Community Access

The intention of this licence is to preserve free and independent access to SIP materials.

No individual, service provider or organization may use this licence as a basis to claim exclusive control over SIP materials or prevent others from exercising the permissions granted here.

## 12. Interpretation

This licence should be interpreted in a manner consistent with its stated purpose:

**SIP materials are freely shared resources for use, adaptation and community benefit. They are not products to be bought, sold or controlled through commercial resale.**

The permission to charge for professional services must not be interpreted as permission to sell SIP itself.
